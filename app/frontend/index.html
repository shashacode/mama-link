URGENT HELP
Call 112 for a real emergency in Nigeria
The Nigerian Communications Commission assigns 112 as the national Emergency Communication Centre number for ambulance, fire, police, FRSC and NEMA emergencies. Connection and response availability can vary.
Do not wait for this demo. Seek immediate in-person help at an appropriate healthcare facility.
