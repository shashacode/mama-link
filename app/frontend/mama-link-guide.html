When to seek help urgently

Heavy bleeding, trouble breathing, chest pain, a persistent severe headache, a seizure or reduced baby movement need urgent attention. Seek immediate in-person care. Do not wait for an app or chat reply.

In Nigeria, call 112 for an emergency. Connection and response availability vary.

You do not need a device

Tell a health worker what you feel, when it began, and whether it is changing. It is okay not to know your blood pressure, temperature or exact pregnancy week. Do not guess a measurement.

Care after birth

Complications can happen after delivery. Tell a healthcare professional if you recently gave birth. Fever, bad-smelling discharge and overwhelming tiredness deserve attention.

Medicines and supplements

Talk to your healthcare professional before starting, stopping or changing a medicine, including herbal products. Bring the medicine name, dose, other medicines and any allergies. An app cannot decide whether a medicine is safe for you.

Questions to take to your visit

  • What changes should I seek help for?
  • Where can I get care if the clinic is closed?
  • What is my next appointment?
  • Who can review my medicines and supplements?
  • What support is available for transport or costs?
