## WHO: maternal mortality

Source: World Health Organization, “Maternal mortality” fact sheet.
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality

Retrieved: 2026-09-18

WHO describes major complications associated with maternal deaths as including
severe bleeding, infections, high blood pressure during pregnancy, delivery
complications, and unsafe abortion. The fact sheet stresses access to skilled
care before, during, and after childbirth. This summary supports education and
source navigation only; it does not define MAMA-Link's screening rules.

## WHO: danger signs in pregnancy

Source: World Health Organization, *Counselling for Maternal and Newborn Health
Care: A Handbook for Building Skills*.
https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/44016/9789241547628_eng.pdf

Retrieved: 2026-09-18

The handbook contains a section on danger signs in pregnancy and emphasizes
prompt care-seeking when danger signs occur. Users should open the source and
follow current local clinical guidance. This corpus does not turn the handbook
into an automated diagnosis or treatment protocol.

## UNFPA: obstetric fistula

Source: United Nations Population Fund, “Obstetric fistula.”
https://www.unfpa.org/obstetric-fistula

Retrieved: 2026-09-18

UNFPA describes obstetric fistula as a severe childbirth injury associated with
prolonged, obstructed labour and lack of timely, high-quality medical treatment.
The page covers prevention, treatment, rehabilitation, and social effects. This
summary is educational and must not be used to diagnose an individual.
